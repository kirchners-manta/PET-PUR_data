# PET-PUR_data
Here we provide the data corresponding to the "Swelling in the Eye of Secondary Interactions: Polyethylene Terephthalate and Polyurethane Nanoplastic Particles in Various Media" manuscript.


## Folder Structure
In the folder named **energy-min**, you will find the CHARMM-ff energy minimized structures of the last simulation step for each of the systems studied, for 20 and 50ns. 
Additionally, all MakroLyzer analyses for the corresponding structures are contained in these folders, as well as the potential energy files.

The folders **SA-traj** and **Equi-traj-solv** have the same structure. 
They contain the MakroLyzer analyses for the whole trajectories of the investigated systems. 
In **SA-traj** you can find the analyses of the initial simulated annealing trajectories for constructing the particles in the gas phase. 
**Equi-traj-solv** corresponds to the analyses of the dissolution trajectories of only PET and PUR in three different solvents (H2O, DMF and TFA).
